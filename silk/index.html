Questions

Not sure it suits your skin? Email us a photo and a skincare advisor will reply.

Does it actually get cold?
Yes — the tip reaches about 10°C within seconds of pressing the cold button, and holds it for the six-minute cycle.
Is it safe for sensitive skin?
The cold settings are gentler than ice. Start on 14°C. Don't use on broken skin.
Can I use it with my serum?
That's what warm mode is for — apply, then glide at 38–42°C.

How to use

  1. Hold the snowflake button: cold in seconds.
  2. Glide from the inner eye outward, then along the jaw. Three minutes a side.
  3. Evenings: apply serum, hold the sun button, warm mode over the face.
Specifications
Cold 10 / 12 / 14°C · Warm 38 / 42°C · 6-minute auto-off · ~35 min per charge · USB-C · 180 g · aluminium tip, matte handle.
Safety
Not for use on broken skin, during migraine aura, or with implanted electronics near the face. Wipe the tip after each use.
