Potting up

  1. Drop the wick core in, thread the wick through the base.
  2. Add soil and the plant. Water from the top once.
  3. Fill the reservoir to the line. Check the window weekly.
Which size?

12 cm for cuttings and herbs · 18 cm for most houseplants · 24 cm for monstera, fiddle-leaf, trees under 1 m.

Care

Rinse the reservoir every few months. Dishwasher safe without the wick.
